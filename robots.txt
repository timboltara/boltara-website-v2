# Everything is crawlable on purpose.
#
# The booking confirmation page is kept out of search by a
# "noindex, nofollow" meta tag on the page itself, NOT by a Disallow here.
# Those two do not combine: a Disallow stops the crawler from ever reading
# the page, so it never sees the noindex, and the URL can still show up in
# results as a bare link from anywhere that points at it. Allowing the
# crawl is what lets the noindex actually work.
