## 1. Hook (0:00–0:15)
"If you run a service business — sandblasting, junk removal, coatings, pool work, whatever it is — you already know the real problem isn't the work. It's that some months your phone rings off the hook, and some months it doesn't ring at all."

## 2. Agitate (0:15–0:40)
"Most service businesses are still running on word-of-mouth and referrals. That works — until it doesn't. You can't scale a schedule you don't control. And when a lead does come in from a boosted post or a half-built ad account, by the time you call them back, they've already hired someone else."

## 3. Reframe / Introduce the system (0:40–1:10)
"That's the actual problem we solve. Not 'we run Facebook ads.' We build the entire pipeline — the ads that bring in the right kind of customer, the follow-up that works every lead so none of them slip through, and the booking system that puts a qualified appointment straight on your calendar. You show up, quote the job, close it. That's it."

## 4. Proof (1:10–2:00)
[Cut to on-screen stat cards — reuse the same results graphics as the site's Results section]

"Here's what that's actually done. Michael runs a wet blasting business — he turned $1,178 in ad spend into $5,100 in closed jobs in his first 40 days. Jeremy runs a sandblasting business — $436 in spend turned into $4,300 already scheduled, plus another $91,100 in quotes sent in 20 days. Bryce runs a junk removal business — $452 turned into $2,970 closed in 60 days. Three completely different service businesses, same system, real numbers. Across our clients, that's an average 5.68x return on ad spend over the last 60 days."

## 5. Differentiate (2:00–2:25)
"We're not a generic marketing agency running the same playbook for every industry. We work with service businesses specifically — we know what a good lead looks like for a trade, we know your ticket sizes, and we build campaigns and follow-up around how your business actually runs, not a template."

## 6. CTA (2:25–end)
"If you want to see exactly what this would look like for your business, book a call below. It's a 20-minute conversation — we'll look at your current lead flow and give you a straight answer on whether this makes sense. No pressure, no generic pitch deck."
