Before we get on the call

Three minutes of prep and we can spend the whole call on your shop instead of on background.

01
Know roughly what a job is worth to you
Your average ticket and your best month. We build the numbers backward from what a job actually pays you, so a rough figure is enough.
02
Know how much work you could take on tomorrow, and what kind
If we turned the phone on this week, how many more jobs could you actually run without dropping quality? That number sets the pace. Then name the work you actually want more of. Almost every owner tells us "I can do everything," and that is the one answer we cannot build ads around. Pick the two or three job types you want the calendar full of and we point the money there.
03
Have your photos somewhere you can find them
Before and afters off your phone. Real photos of your own work outperform anything else we can run, and it is the one thing only you can give us. Shots of you or your crew on the job site with your face in them convert the best of anything we put in an ad, so grab those too if you have them.
04
Take the call somewhere you can talk
Not from the blast booth. We go through your numbers on screen, so a spot where you can look at a screen for 45 minutes helps.
